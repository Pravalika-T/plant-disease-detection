Plant Health AI
Project Overview

Empowering Farmers with Artificial Intelligence

The AI-Based Plant Disease Detection System is a college project designed to help farmers detect crop diseases early using leaf photos.

By combining Deep Learning (Convolutional Neural Networks) with a simple, easy-to-use interface, our goal is to eliminate agricultural crop losses caused by delayed disease detection.

Project Purpose

To provide farmers with a instant, reliable, and accessible tool that identifies plant health issues and recommends organic and biological treatments without technical complexity.

System Architecture

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap 5
  • Backend: Java, Spring Boot REST APIs
  • Database: MySQL
  • AI Module: Python, TensorFlow/Keras & OpenCV
