How It Works

Detecting plant diseases is as easy as taking a photo. Follow these 3 simple steps.

Step 1

Upload Photo

Take or upload a clear, focused photo of the affected plant leaf. Make sure the leaf spots or marks are visible.

Step 2

AI Check

Our smart AI system analyzes the leaf texture, color, and spot patterns instantly to identify potential health issues.

Step 3

Get Result

Receive your plant health diagnosis immediately along with easy-to-follow treatment and prevention steps.

Tips for Best Accuracy

Use bright natural daylight when taking the picture.
Keep the leaf flat and centered in the image.
Avoid dark shadows or blurry background images.
Focus directly on leaf spots or discolored areas.
